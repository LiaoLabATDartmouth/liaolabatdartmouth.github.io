We are actively recruiting motivated scientists at all levels, including graduate students, postdoctoral fellows, research assistants, and lab technicians.

Prospective graduate students are encouraged to apply through either the [Molecular and Cellular Biology Graduate Program](https://graduate.dartmouth.edu/mcb/) or the [Quantitative Biomedical Sciences Graduate Program](https://geiselmed.dartmouth.edu/qbs/). Chen is affiliated with both programs and can mentor students from either one. Interested students are welcome to contact Chen by email to discuss potential research directions and fit with the lab.

We welcome inquiries from individuals interested in research assistant or lab technician positions. Interested applicants should email Chen to ask about current openings, potential projects, and funding opportunities. Please include a brief description of your research interests, relevant experience, and a CV or resume.

Postdoctoral applicants should send a cover letter and CV to chen.liao AT dartmouth.edu. The cover letter should describe your research background, your interest in the lab, and potential project areas you would like to pursue. Please also include the names and contact information of references.
